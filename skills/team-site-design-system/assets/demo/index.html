Design system

Restrained, document-like, Apple greys

Colour tokens

Every colour comes from a token. Light and dark share names; dark is a soft grey, never pure black.

Member and calendar colours

Google Calendar's eleven event colours, mapped to Apple system colours (--gc-1 … --gc-11).
