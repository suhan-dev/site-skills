## Learned · results
In steady state the temperature along the rod falls off in a straight line when the side is insulated. With heat loss
from the side it bends:

$$\frac{d^2 T}{dx^2} = m^2 \left(T - T_\infty\right), \qquad m^2 = \frac{h P}{k A}$$

where $k$ is the conductivity and $h$ the loss to the air.
