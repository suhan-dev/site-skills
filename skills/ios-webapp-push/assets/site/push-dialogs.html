App & notifications

On iPhone iOS 16.4 or later
  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. KakaoTalk can't add apps to the Home Screen. Open this page in Safari first. Open in Safari Or tap ⋯ → Open in Safari.
  3. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  4. Open the app from your Home Screen.
  5. Tap the bell → Turn on notifications → Allow.

Add the app to your Home Screen

Add it as an app to get notifications.

  1. Tap Share. If it isn't there, tap ⋯ first.
  2. KakaoTalk can't add apps. Open this page in Safari first. Open in Safari
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  5. Keep Open as Web App on if it shows, then tap Add.
  6. Open the app from your Home Screen.
